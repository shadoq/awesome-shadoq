# Design resources

[Back to the index](../README.md)

## In short

A developer does not need to be a designer, but needs a short list of sources for images, icons, fonts, sounds and patterns, and the right to use them.

- **Check the license every time.** CC0 and public domain (Openclipart, Foodiesfeed, Pixabay) are safest; "free for personal use" is not free for a product.
- **Patterns before pixels.** UI-Patterns, Landingfolio and the Game UI Database show how others solved the same screen.
- **Tools:** Canva for quick graphics, UXPin for prototypes, pngquant to shrink PNGs, Coolors for palettes.
- **Sound:** Kevin MacLeod and the Free Music Archive for music, freeSFX for effects; credit the authors as their licenses require.

**Where to start:** Unsplash, Pixabay, UI-Patterns.

## Stock photos and images

- [Foodiesfeed](http://foodiesfeed.com/) - Free CC0 high-resolution food photos for commercial use without attribution.
- [Freerange Stock](http://freerangestock.com/) - Free stock photos, illustrations and textures, legally sourced for commercial use.
- [Openclipart](http://openclipart.org/) - Public domain clipart and vector graphics created by artists, free to reuse.
- [picjumbo](http://picjumbo.com/) - Free stock photos and backgrounds for commercial and personal projects.
- [Pixabay](http://pixabay.com/) - Large library of free images, illustrations and videos under a permissive license.
- [Textures.com (CG Textures)](http://cgtextures.com/) - Large library of textures and materials for 3D, graphic design and Photoshop.
- [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/) - Free high-resolution stock photos under the Unsplash license, widely used in web design.
- [Death to Stock](http://deathtothestockphoto.com/) - Stock photo and video membership library; the site describes its imagery as creator-made, not AI.
- [EveryStockPhoto](http://www.everystockphoto.com/) - Search engine that aggregates free stock photos from several providers.
- [Freepik (Magnific)](https://www.freepik.com/) - Stock graphics and AI image, video and audio tools, now rebranded as Magnific.
- [Freepixels](http://www.freepixels.com/) - Free stock photos and images for personal and commercial projects, no attribution required.
- [Generated Photos](https://generated.photos/) - Stock library of AI-generated headshot and model photos.
- [Image After](http://www.imageafter.com/) - Collection of free textures and images for design and 3D work.
- [Life of Pix](http://www.lifeofpix.com/) - Free high-resolution photography with search by colour and format.
- [Little Visuals](http://littlevisuals.co/) - Free high-resolution images released weekly, usable for commercial projects.
- [New Old Stock](http://nos.twnsnd.co/) - Vintage photos from public archives, collected for reuse with no known copyright restrictions.
- [Pixnio (public-domain-image.com)](http://www.public-domain-image.com/) - Public domain images and royalty-free photos for personal and commercial use, no attribution needed.
- [SplitShire](http://splitshire.com/) - Free stock photos and videos for personal and commercial use.
- [TESTIMAGES](https://testimages.org/) - Free collection of digital test images.
- [Transparent Textures](https://www.transparenttextures.com/) - Free seamless background textures for designers.

## Design tools

- [Canva](https://www.canva.com/) - Online design platform for presentations, social graphics and marketing visuals.
- [pngquant](https://pngquant.org/) - Lossy PNG compressor for reducing image file size.
- [UXPin](https://www.uxpin.com/) - UX and UI design tool for interactive prototypes, with code-based components and design systems.
- [ClipDrop](https://clipdrop.co/) - AI image tools for background removal, cleanup and upscaling.
- [Lovart](https://www.lovart.ai/) - AI design agent that generates logos, packaging and marketing visuals from a prompt.
- [Pixlr](https://pixlr.com/pl) - Browser-based photo editor with AI image generation and batch editing.
- [PixTeller](https://pixteller.com/) - Online image editor for creating posters, graphics, animated videos and banners.
- [PostSpark](https://postspark.app/) - Screenshot editor for backgrounds, frames and arrows, exporting PNG, JPG or MP4.
- [Shots.so](https://shots.so/) - Tool for creating device and app mockup images for product screenshots.
- [Snappa](https://snappa.com/) - Online graphic design tool for social posts, blog images and ads.
- [Stitch (formerly Galileo AI)](https://www.usegalileo.ai/) - AI tool that generates UI designs for mobile and web applications.
- [SVG Viewer](https://www.svgviewer.dev/) - Online tool to view, edit and optimize SVG files.
- [VistaCreate](https://create.vista.com/pl) - Online graphic design tool with a large template library.
- [withoutbg](https://github.com/withoutbg/withoutbg) - Python SDK for local and cloud background removal from images, with open API models.

## UI design and patterns

- [Android Design guidelines](http://developer.android.com/design/index.html) - Official Android guidance for designing interfaces and app architecture.
- [Awwwards](http://www.awwwards.com/) - Website awards and showcase for web design and development work.
- [Game UI Database](https://www.gameuidatabase.com/) - Screenshot library of video game user interfaces for designers.
- [Landingfolio](https://www.landingfolio.com/) - Curated gallery of landing page designs, templates and UI components.
- [UI-Patterns.com](http://ui-patterns.com/) - Library of user interface and persuasive design patterns with real-world examples.
- [awesome-design-md](https://github.com/voltagent/awesome-design-md) - Collection of DESIGN.md files describing popular brand design systems for AI agents.
- [awesome-react-design-systems](https://github.com/jbranchaud/awesome-react-design-systems) - Curated collection of React-based design systems.
- [Beyond Generative: agentic AI and UX (Smashing)](https://www.smashingmagazine.com/2026/01/beyond-generative-rise-agentic-ai-user-centric-design) - Article on UX research for agentic AI, focusing on trust, consent and accountability.
- [SaaS Landing Page](https://saaslandingpage.com/) - Gallery of landing page examples from SaaS companies for design inspiration.
- [Smashing Magazine](http://www.smashingmagazine.com/tag/mobile) - Magazine with articles on front-end, UX and design for developers and designers.

## Audio assets

- [Free Music Archive](https://freemusicarchive.org/) - Large library of royalty-free and Creative Commons music with licensing details.
- [Kevin MacLeod royalty-free music](http://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free) - Royalty-free music by Kevin MacLeod, released under Creative Commons Attribution.
- [AudionautiX](http://www.audionautix.com/) - Free Creative Commons production music by Jason Shaw for podcasts, videos and business use.
- [ccMixter](http://dig.ccmixter.org/free_music) - Creative Commons music library with tracks free for commercial use under various CC licenses.
- [Free-Loops.com](http://free-loops.com/) - Free drum loops, samples and MIDI sound loops for music production.
- [freeSFX.co.uk](http://www.freesfx.co.uk/) - Free sound effects for games and multimedia projects.
- [Looperman](http://www.looperman.com/) - Community site for free loops, samples, acapellas and sounds from music producers.
- [NoCopyrightSounds](https://ncs.io/) - Label offering free copyright-free electronic music for creators.

## Icons, fonts and color

- [ColorZilla Gradient Editor](http://www.colorzilla.com/gradient-editor) - Online CSS gradient editor with live preview and copy-ready code.
- [DaFont](http://www.dafont.com/) - Archive of freely downloadable fonts, browsable by style, author and popularity.
- [theSVG](https://thesvg.org/) - Open-source library of brand SVG icons with npm, React, Vue, Svelte, CLI and CDN access.
- [Color Wizard](http://www.colorsontheweb.com/colorwizard.asp) - Online tool that builds harmonious colour palettes from a base colour.
- [Coolors](https://coolors.co/) - Web tool for generating and browsing color palettes.
- [DCL colormaps](http://ruby.gfd-dennou.org/products/ruby-dcl/colormaps) - Colormaps for the Ruby-DCL scientific visualization library.
- [Iconfinder](http://www.iconfinder.com/) - Icon search engine; the link now redirects to magnific.com/icons.
- [typografia.info](http://typografia.info/index.php?option=com_content&task=view&id=6&Itemid=4) - Polish-language site on typography in general.
