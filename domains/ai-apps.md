# AI apps for everyday work

[Back to the index](../README.md)

## In short

End-user AI apps age fast: many of the ones bookmarked two years ago are gone. The ones that last either do one job very well or give you control over which model you use.

- **Research with sources:** NotebookLM answers from your own documents; Perplexity searches the web and cites. Both beat a bare chatbot for anything you need to check.
- **Bring your own model:** desktop clients such as Jan and Cherry Studio talk to local models and many APIs from one window, so you are not locked into one subscription.
- **Writing:** LanguageTool is open source and handles Polish well; most "AI copywriting" tools are thin wrappers over the same models.
- **Directories** (There's An AI For That, Futurepedia) are good for discovery but not for judging quality; treat listings as leads, not recommendations.

**Rule of thumb:** before paying for an AI app, check whether a general assistant with a good prompt already does the job.

## Writing and research

- [LanguageTool](https://languagetool.org/pl) - Open-source grammar and style checker for many languages, including Polish, with a free tier.
- [NotebookLM](https://notebooklm.google/) - Google AI research tool that answers questions and summarizes from user-provided sources.
- [Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai/) - AI answer engine that searches the web and cites sources.
- [DesktopTranslator](https://github.com/ymoslem/DesktopTranslator) - Local cross-platform desktop GUI for machine translation, built on CTranslate2.
- [Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com/) - AI writing assistant that checks and improves text in apps and websites.
- [Jasper](https://www.jasper.ai/) - AI copywriting platform for marketing teams.
- [Resoomer](https://resoomer.com/pl) - Online text summarizer and document reader with a free tier.
- [SciSpace (Typeset)](https://typeset.io/) - AI assistant for reading and asking questions about scientific PDFs.
- [Unstuck AI](https://unstuckstudy.com/) - AI study tool that turns PDFs, slides, videos and notes into chat-based study aids.
- [Writesonic](https://writesonic.com/) - AI writing tool with SEO content and AI-search visibility features.
- [Zapytaj Kodeks](https://zapytajkodeks.pl/) - Polish AI assistant for searching legal provisions against current law sources.

## Assistants and chat clients

- [Cherry Studio](https://github.com/CherryHQ/cherry-studio) - Open-source desktop client for chatting with many LLM providers, with agents and assistants.
- [Jan](https://jan.ai/) - Open-source desktop app for running LLMs locally or connecting to cloud models.
- [1min.AI](https://1min.ai/) - All-in-one AI app for text, image, audio and video generation.
- [big-AGI](https://github.com/enricoros/big-agi) - Open-source AI chat application supporting multiple models, personas, voice and image generation.
- [Intel AI Playground](https://game.intel.com/no/stories/introducing-ai-playground) - Intel's generative AI app suite for AI PCs, covering image, chat and model tools.
- [odysseus](https://github.com/pewdiepie-archdaemon/odysseus) - Self-hosted workspace combining AI chat, agents, research, documents, email, notes and local models.
- [rowboat](https://github.com/rowboatlabs/rowboat) - Open-source AI coworker with memory and collaboration features.
- [Second-Me](https://github.com/Mindverse/Second-Me) - Open-source project for training a personal AI model of yourself.
- [Sesame](https://www.sesame.com/) - Conversational voice AI assistant with mobile apps.
- [Venice.ai](https://venice.ai/) - Privacy-focused AI platform for generating text, images, characters and video.

## Productivity and work

- [Gamma](https://gamma.app/) - AI tool for creating presentations, websites and documents from prompts.
- [Genspark](https://www.genspark.ai/) - AI workspace that uses agents to produce documents, slides and research.
- [Marpit](https://marpit.marp.app/) - Framework for creating slide decks from Markdown, used by Marp.
- [Motion](https://www.usemotion.com/) - AI productivity app combining tasks, calendar, docs and meetings.
- [probly](https://github.com/PragmaticMachineLearning/probly) - AI-assisted spreadsheet app with Python execution, charts and data extraction from documents. MIT licensed.
- [TinyWow](https://tinywow.com/) - Free online tools for PDF, image and AI writing tasks.
