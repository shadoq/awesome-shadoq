# Marketing and SEO

[Back to the index](../README.md)

## In short

SEO and marketing tools fall into three jobs: see what people search for, see what competitors do, and measure what you get.

- **Search demand:** Google Trends and AnswerThePublic show what people ask; Google Search Console shows what you already rank for and is the first tool to set up.
- **Competitors and ads:** the Meta Ad Library shows every active ad on Facebook and Instagram; Ahrefs and Moz show who links to whom.
- **Email:** mail-tester tells you why newsletters land in spam; Mautic is the open-source marketing automation option.
- **Social:** Typefully for drafting and scheduling posts; Postiz as a self-hosted alternative.
- **Old SEO knowledge still holds:** relevance, links and technical health. Many tools here are older, but their principles are unchanged.

**Where to start:** Google Search Console and Google Trends, both free.

## Market research

- [Google Trends](http://google.pl/trends) - Google tool showing relative search interest over time and by region.
- [Meta Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/) - Public archive of active ads on Meta platforms for ad research.
- [AnswerThePublic](https://answerthepublic.com/ombwqq/searches) - Keyword research tool that visualizes questions people ask around a topic.
- [eMarketer](http://www.emarketer.com/Welcome.aspx) - Market research and statistics on internet marketing, advertising and demographics.
- [Exploding Topics](https://explodingtopics.com/) - Tool for discovering trending topics and emerging market opportunities.
- [gemiusRanking PL](http://ranking.pl/) - Polish web audience measurement and ranking of website traffic.
- [HubSpot Make My Persona](https://www.hubspot.com/make-my-persona) - Free buyer persona generator tool by HubSpot.
- [leadlista.pl](https://leadlista.pl/) - Polish site showing which websites use platforms like Shoper, WooCommerce and PrestaShop.
- [Marketing przy Kawie](http://www.marketing-news.pl/) - Polish marketing guide covering trends, campaign results and practical lessons.
- [SimilarSites](http://www.similarsites.com/) - Finds websites similar to a given domain, for competitor and market research.
- [Sprawny Marketing](http://www.sprawnymarketing.pl/) - Polish marketing training site with a magazine, conferences and tool tests.

## SEO tools

- [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console/about) - Google tool for monitoring search performance, indexing and site health.
- [Moz](http://www.seomoz.org/) - SEO software and learning resources for search and content marketing.
- [SEOBook](http://www.seobook.com/) - SEO tools, training and guides by Aaron Wall.
- [Advanced Web Ranking](http://www.advancedwebranking.com/) - Rank tracking software for SEO and AI search visibility with client reporting.
- [Copyscape](http://www.copyscape.com/) - Online plagiarism and duplicate content checker for web pages and text.
- [iSearchFrom](https://isearchfrom.com/) - Tool to simulate Google searches from different locations, languages and devices.
- [Lexy SEO blog](http://www.lexy.com.pl/blog) - Polish SEO blog with articles and SEO course materials by Marta Gryszko.
- [Local search ranking factors (D. Mihm)](http://www.davidmihm.com/local-search-ranking-factors.shtml) - Study by David Mihm on local search ranking factors for Google Maps and Yahoo Local.
- [SEOStudio](https://seostudio.tools/) - Collection of free online SEO tools in one place.
- [WebCEO](http://www.webceo.com/) - All-in-one SEO software suite with rank tracking, audits and link tools.
- [WmTools](https://wmtools.me/) - Free webmaster utilities for SEO analysis, domain lookup and password generation.

- [Brand24](http://brand24.pl/) - Social media and web mention monitoring for brands, with sentiment analysis.
- [Brandwatch](http://www.brandwatch.com/) - Enterprise social media management and consumer intelligence suite.
- [Mastodon](https://joinmastodon.org/pl) - Decentralized, open-source social network run on independent servers.
- [postiz-app](https://github.com/gitroomhq/postiz-app) - Open-source social media scheduling tool with AI features for planning and publishing posts.

- [Social Engine](https://pl.wordpress.org/plugins/social-engine) - WordPress plugin for scheduling social media posts, self-hosted, with MCP support.
- [Sotrender](http://www.sotrender.com/pl) - Polish social media analytics and reporting platform for Facebook and other channels.
- [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/) - YouTube analytics and SEO tool for growing channel views and subscribers.

## Marketing automation and sales

- [mail-tester](https://www.mail-tester.com/) - Free tool that checks newsletter emails for spam-filter problems.
- [Mautic (GitHub)](https://github.com/mautic/mautic) - Open-source marketing automation software repository.
- [Ad Mockup Generator](https://admockups.com/) - Free tool for creating ad mockups quickly.
- [Apollo.io](https://www.apollo.io/) - Sales intelligence platform with contact data and outreach tools for go-to-market teams.
- [PhantomBuster](https://phantombuster.com/) - Automation tool for social network data extraction and lead tasks.
- [SendGrid](https://sendgrid.com/en-us) - Email API and marketing campaigns platform, now part of Twilio.
- [Twilio Segment](https://segment.com/) - Customer data platform that collects event data from apps and routes it to other tools.

## Backlinks and domains

- [Ahrefs](http://ahrefs.com/) - Commercial SEO platform for backlink analysis, keyword research and rank tracking.
- [Moz Link Explorer](http://www.opensiteexplorer.org/) - Backlink explorer with Domain Authority metrics and linking domains; free tier available.
- [Backlink Watch](http://www.backlinkwatch.com/) - Free backlink checker showing pages that link to a given site.
- [dns.pl deleted_domains.txt](http://dns.pl/deleted_domains.txt) - Text file listing recently deleted .pl domains, for finding expired domains to register.
- [Link-Assistant free backlink checker](http://webmeup.com/) - Free backlink checker showing which pages link to a site, with anchor and new or lost link data.
- [MapaDomen.pl](http://mapadomen.pl/) - Searchable list of .pl domains removed from the registry.
