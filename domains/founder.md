# Building a product and a small software business

[Back to the index](../README.md)

## In short

Building a small software business is less about tools and more about a few durable ideas.

- **Small and specific wins.** Kevin Kelly's 1,000 True Fans is the classic argument that a narrow, loyal audience is enough. Indie Hackers shows real revenue numbers from people doing it.
- **Measure from day one, cheaply.** Plausible or Matomo for privacy-friendly analytics, Microsoft Clarity for free session recordings, Google Analytics when you need the standard.
- **Launch where builders look:** Product Hunt, Indie Hackers products and smaller directories. A launch is a spike, not a strategy.
- **Do not pay for what is free or open:** open-source SaaS alternatives and European alternatives lists help cut costs and keep data in the EU. ActivityWatch tracks your own time locally.
- **Stay informed, selectively:** The Pragmatic Engineer for the engineering side, Hacker News for the pulse.

**Where to start:** 1,000 True Fans, then Indie Hackers.

## Business tools

- [ActivityWatch](https://activitywatch.net/) - Open-source, local-first automatic time tracker for Windows, macOS, Linux and Android.
- [European Alternatives](https://european-alternatives.eu/alternatives-to) - Directory of European alternatives to popular cloud services and SaaS products.
- [Linear](https://linear.app/) - Issue tracking and product planning tool used by software teams, with AI agent support.
- [40 open-source alternatives to SaaS (Indie Hackers)](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/40-open-source-gems-to-replace-your-saas-subscriptions-f733b8e166) - Indie Hackers post listing 40 open-source tools that can replace SaaS subscriptions.
- [awesome-saas-services](https://github.com/GetStream/awesome-saas-services) - Curated list of SaaS services for developers and business owners.
- [Fair-code](https://faircode.io/) - Sustainable software licensing model described by the fair-code initiative.
- [G2](https://www.g2.com/) - Software review and comparison marketplace for business tools.
- [Open Source Alternatives](https://www.opensourcealternative.to/) - Directory of open-source alternatives to proprietary software.
- [PARP online courses](https://www.parp.gov.pl/component/site/site/kursy-online) - Online courses for small and medium businesses from PARP, a Polish government agency.
- [Secret](https://www.joinsecret.com/) - Software discount platform offering startups and founders deals on business tools.

## Communities, newsletters and essays

- [1,000 True Fans](https://kk.org/thetechnium/1000-true-fans) - Kevin Kelly essay on how creators can earn a living from a small base of dedicated fans.
- [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/) - Community for founders sharing revenue, growth and indie product stories.
- [The Pragmatic Engineer](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/) - Gergely Orosz newsletter on big tech, startups and engineering careers.
- [Arm Yourself With Specific Knowledge](https://nav.al/specific-knowledge) - Naval Ravikant essay on building specific knowledge that cannot be outsourced.
- [Bogusz Pekalski mailing](https://akademiasaas.mailingr.co/p/mailing) - Free newsletter by a Polish founder about building and running a SaaS.
- [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/) - Tech news aggregator and discussion forum run by Y Combinator.
- [Marc Lou indie page](https://marclou.com/) - Personal site of indie hacker Marc Lou, documenting solo startup projects.
- [OpenSourceDiscovery](https://opensourcedisc.substack.com/) - Biweekly newsletter curating useful open-source projects.
- [Startups for the Rest of Us](https://www.startupsfortherestofus.com/) - Startup podcast with over 750 episodes.
- [The Hustle](https://thehustle.co/) - Daily business and tech newsletter covering startups and innovation stories.

## Freelance

- [Czy Jest Eldorado?](https://czyjesteldorado.pl/) - Aggregator of IT job offers from many Polish job portals.
- [Freelancer.com](https://www.freelancer.com/) - Global marketplace for posting freelance projects and hiring freelancers.
- [freelancermap](https://www.freelancermap.com/) - Marketplace for freelance IT projects and contracts.
- [Kimai](https://www.kimai.org/) - Open-source time tracking with invoicing for freelancers and agencies.
- [midday](https://github.com/midday-ai/midday) - Open-source freelancer platform: invoicing, time tracking, file reconciliation and an AI assistant.
- [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/nx/find-work/best-matches) - Large freelance marketplace for project-based and hourly work.
- [useme.com](https://useme.com/en/jobs) - Freelance job board for projects and remote work.

## Analytics

- [Google Analytics](https://analytics.google.com/analytics/web) - Google's web analytics platform for measuring site traffic and user behaviour.
- [Matomo (formerly Piwik)](http://piwik.org/) - Open source web analytics platform that can be self-hosted, focused on privacy.
- [Microsoft Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) - Free web analytics with session recordings and heatmaps.
- [plausible](https://github.com/plausible/analytics) - Open-source, privacy-first, cookie-free web analytics and Google Analytics alternative.
- [PostHog startups program](https://posthog.com/startups) - PostHog startup program page; describes credits for its product analytics platform.
